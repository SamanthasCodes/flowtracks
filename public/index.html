About

Every minute, one garbage truck of plastic enters our oceans. This pollution can travel far and wide, from high mountain environments to the deep sea, impacting even the most remote locations. Tracking plastic pollution is essential for understanding where it accumulates, how widely the particles disperse, and how scientists and environmental groups can better target cleanup and prevention efforts.

In the oceans, plastic swiftly flows into a restless system of currents, eddies, and turbulence. Smaller turbulent motions redistribute particles through the surrounding water, shaping how widely pollution disperses, while larger circulation patterns transport debris across long distances and influence where plastic accumulates. Together, these dynamics make it difficult to track plastic's path, model where it accumulates, and implement targeted cleanup interventions.

FlowTracks is an interactive simulation that lets users explore how ocean motion can influence plastic pollution. By releasing virtual particles and adjusting turbulence, users can observe how changes in ocean mixing affect where pollution travels, how widely it spreads, and where it may accumulate. Rather than treating plastic pollution as a distant or invisible problem, FlowTracks invites users to experiment with the dynamics that make ocean plastic difficult to track, model, and manage.

How to Use

FlowTracks lets you release virtual plastic particles into the ocean and watch how currents and turbulence carry them across the globe.

Click anywhere on the ocean to release a cloud of particles. Each particle is a virtual tracer representing a piece of floating plastic. For the best experience, release one cloud at a time and allow it to drift before clicking again. The particle spread remains on screen until you press Reset. Clicking on land will not release particles.

Controls
Diffusionadjusts random small-scale turbulence; higher values make the particle cloud spread faster and farther.
Lifetimeadjusts how long the particle cloud remains visible; longer lifetimes allow the particle cloud to visibly drift farther across the oceans.
Particle Countadjusts how many virtual tracer particles are released per click; higher counts create a denser view of the spreading pattern.
Currentscontrols ocean current labels; toggle on to identify large-scale flows that may influence particle movement.
Retina Resolutioncontrols canvas sharpness for high-resolution screens; toggle off to enhance performance if simulation runs slowly.
The Map

The country colors show projected plastic marine debris entering the ocean in 2025 (tonnes per year). Hover over any country to see its name and estimated contribution. The particle density heatmap shows where particles have concentrated over time.

The Spread Chart

The sidebar chart tracks how far your particle cloud spreads over time. Each new release appears as a separate colored line. Press Reset to clear all releases and start fresh.
